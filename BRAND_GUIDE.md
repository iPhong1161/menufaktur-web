## Brand summary

menufaktur turns cooking into a genuine craft anyone can master, and shared meals into moments of true connection. Founded in 2007 in Frankfurt-Bornheim, menufaktur guides private guests and corporate teams to cook full menus from start to finish under calm expert hands, then sit together as equals.

The brand stands for:
- **Craft over spectacle** — real technique, fresh ingredients, honest flavours over gimmicks
- **Connection over consumption** — through shared learning and success
- **Quiet confidence** — like a thoughtful host who quietly makes everything perfect

## Unique selling points

- Guests actively cook the full multi-course menu themselves — hands-on craft guided by professionals, not just watching or show cooking
- Nearly 20 years of proven reliability (since 2007) — established, trustworthy alternative to trendy or untested concepts
- Perfect hybrid: equally excellent for relaxed private evenings (friends, couples, families) and high-value corporate team events / client hosting
- Warm, personal backyard location in Frankfurt-Bornheim — urban yet welcoming, never chaotic, never cold or sterile
- Grown-up, calm brand personality + future-ready digital ecosystem (seamless booking, digital recipes, vouchers, CRM) that feels premium without pretension

## Tone of voice

**Personality:** Calm, confident, welcoming, clear, warmly professional.

**Voice:** Like an experienced, generous host — knowing, reassuring, never making anyone feel small. Warm yet not effusive. Professional yet not stiff. Friendly yet never loud or hype-filled.

**Writing principles:**
- Explain clearly
- Use active voice for hands-on moments ("You will cook…")
- Subtle natural humour if it fits, but professionalism comes first
- Writing should feel like a perfectly set table: elegant, inviting, quietly impressive

**Avoid:**
- Hype words ("amazing," "incredible," "game-changing")
- Excessive exclamation marks
- Overly casual or slangy phrasing
- Marketing clichés
- Anything that sounds like it's trying too hard

## Typography

The brand uses the **Roboto** type family across weights:

| Use | Font | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Headings (title case) | Roboto Bold | Bold |
| Alt-headings (title case) | Roboto Bold | Bold |
| Sub-headings (title case) | Roboto Regular | Regular |
| Body text | Roboto Condensed Light | Light |

**Hierarchy guidelines for web:**
- H1 (page title): Roboto Bold, large, generous spacing
- H2 (section): Roboto Bold, smaller than H1
- H3 (sub-section): Roboto Regular, even smaller, may use color accent
- Body: Roboto Condensed Light or Roboto Regular for readability — Condensed Light is elegant but should be tested for legibility at small sizes; Roboto Regular is a safe fallback for paragraph text
- Buttons / CTAs: Roboto Bold

Roboto is available free via Google Fonts: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto and Roboto Condensed: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto+Condensed

## Photography style

**Aesthetic:** Calm, mature, quietly premium. Reflects the brand's experienced, welcoming, hands-on, reliable, composed personality.

**Lighting:** Warm, natural, primarily soft diffused daylight. Gently highlights textures, freshness, authentic details. No harsh contrasts.

**Composition:** Simple and unpretentious. Captures genuine moments of:
- People cooking full menus together
- Fresh ingredients
- Handcrafted dishes
- Shared warmth around the table
- The urban backyard setting in Frankfurt-Bornheim

**Style notes:**
- Generous negative space
- Clean framing
- Restrained depth of field
- Effortless, high-quality, free from over-styling or gimmicks
- Color palette in images aligns with brand: deep wine red accents, warm beige neutrals, subtle gold yellow highlights

**Subject matter:**
- Understated close-ups of real ingredients and meals
- Sincere group interactions
- Inviting, lived-in scenes
- Avoid: staged stock photography, harsh studio lighting, over-saturated colors, "lifestyle" clichés

## Application notes for the website

When building the cooking school website, these brand principles translate to:

**Layout & visual feel:**
- Generous white space (or rather, "beige space" — Light Beige is the brand's neutral)
- Warm, confident, never busy or hyped
- Photography is the hero — let images breathe
- Restrained use of color accents

**Buttons & CTAs:**
- Wine Red on Light Beige, or Light Beige on Wine Red
- Clear, calm action labels ("Kurs buchen," "Mehr erfahren") — not "Jetzt zugreifen!!" or hype phrasing

**Microcopy:**
- Welcoming and clear in German
- Active voice where appropriate
- Reassuring tone at decision points (booking, payment)
- Honest about capacity, availability, what's included

**Components:**
- Course cards: warm photo, clear info, calm typography, no aggressive "ONLY 2 LEFT!" urgency tactics — a quiet "noch 4 Plätze" suffices
- Forms: clean, well-spaced, friendly labels
- Confirmations: warm thank-you tone, not transactional
